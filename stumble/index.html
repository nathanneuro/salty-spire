Stumble

A little serendipity engine, in the spirit of the old StumbleUpon.

Hit Stumble and it will show you a painting, a poem, a short story, or a song — pulled live from open archives. Rate what you see with 👍 / 👎 and it learns what you like, nudging future stumbles toward your taste while still wandering enough to surprise you.

Everything it learns stays in your browser. Keys: space stumble  ·  ↑ like  ·  ↓ pass
